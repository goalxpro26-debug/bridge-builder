Grab bricks in your blue, stack them high, and build the bridge across the gorge — before your rivals knock you flying!
20 races · more rivals & longer bridges every race · original game

HOW TO PLAY

Move: drag / hold mouse or finger, or use WASD / arrow keys.

Collect: run over blue bricks to stack them on your head. A big stack makes you slower!

Build: walk up the glowing bridge lane in the middle of the gorge. Each step lays one brick down as a plank.

Watch out: rivals bump into you and scatter your stack. If you run out of bricks mid-gorge — or a rival beats you to the flag — you lose a heart.

Win a race: reach the chequered flag at the top. Lose all 3 hearts and the race is over.

Goal: win all 20 races. Later races have more rivals and much longer bridges.
